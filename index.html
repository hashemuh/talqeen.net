Lesson structure

Every lesson follows the same 7-step sequence

Consistency builds confidence. The same rhythm every time means learners always know what comes next.

1

See

The letter appears large with its harakah (vowel mark) — clear, uncluttered, undistracted.

2

Listen

Hear a certified Qari recite all three positional forms — initial, medial, and final — in sequence.

3

Speak (initial)

Record your voice saying the initial form. On-device AI scores your pronunciation instantly.

4

Speak (medial)

Repeat for the middle position. The app highlights exactly where your pronunciation differs.

5

Speak (final)

Repeat for the end position. Three recordings, three scores, one letter fully understood.

6

Identify

Which shape is initial? Which is medial? Which is final? Recognition before production.

7

Review

All three shapes together, displayed right to left, with audio playback — locking in the complete picture of the letter before the next one begins.
